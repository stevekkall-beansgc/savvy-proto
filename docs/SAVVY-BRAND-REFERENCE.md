# Savvy visual reference — 13 November 2026

The owner asked to use Savvy's style guide, logo and colors, then clarified:
“no - look at their site” when asked about an internal style-guide link.
This implementation follows the official public site. It does not claim access
to Savvy's private design.md or internal product component library.

Sources:

- Official site: https://www.savvywealth.com/
- Official linked stylesheet: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6479cdbae84fd9792129c576/css/savvy-stage.shared.34e7eb512.min.css
- Official wordmark: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6479cdbae84fd9792129c576/66c46822c180a2110276340b_Savvy%20Logo.svg
- Published product design principles: https://www.savvywealth.com/builders-blog-posts/the-5-principles-that-guide-design-at-savvy

The wordmark is used unchanged. Typeface files are the Big Caslon regular and
ABC Diatype regular/medium/bold assets linked by the official site's stylesheet.
They are stored locally so the prototype makes no runtime font requests.
Source URLs are retained in artifacts/brand-reference/selected-assets.json.
These brand assets remain their owners' property; this is a local synthetic
prototype, not a distribution license or an official Savvy product.

Application layout and workflow components are an adaptation, not an extracted
internal design system. Color directs attention to actions and exceptions.
Money totals use neutral ink; charts/bridges use blue, following the published
principle that green should not imply favorable financial performance. Status
color describes evidence/workflow state, never investment returns.

Warm neutrals, the unmodified white wordmark against dark green, Caslon headings,
and Diatype controls replace the earlier Legume visual treatment. Fictional-data
labels remain visible. No real sign-in screen, advisor account or production
connection is implied. Publication remains paused.
