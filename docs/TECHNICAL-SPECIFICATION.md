## 4. Policy semantics

### 4.1 Entities and dimensions

Use canonical `advisor`, optional `book`, and `account`; do not treat broker and custodian as interchangeable. A data observation references a source-scoped account crosswalk and a dated advisor/account attribution only where explicitly supplied. Additional segments, customer or product types are opt-in dimensions with a defined owner, applicable entity grain, allowed values, evidence, effective dates and approval. No dynamic guessing. Preserve `unknown`, `not_provided`, `unmapped`, and `not_applicable` distinctly. Never count an unknown as a wildcard, zero, excluded or matching without policy.

A policy may filter on supported approved dimensions and metrics. Actual segments are invented solely for synthetic examples; no claim about Savvy's classifications. Prevent user-authored arbitrary code and SQL; expose an allowlisted typed expression tree with bounded depth, approved operators, deterministic evaluation and unit checking. Unknown mechanism or incompatible units yields an unresolved exception; no computed zero.

### 4.2 Rate selection

For each eligible component and economic date: candidate rules must be approved, within effective intervals and match the scope. More specific selector supersedes containing less-specific selector. Broker-level promotion supersedes broker standard only while active; a matching more-specific rule supersedes both. Incomparable equal-priority rules (e.g. customer type vs product type) require explicit priority or a more specific intersection rule. Reject conflicting configurations at publish, and hold data-dependent ties during calculation. Explicitly defined equal outputs still require a traceable deterministic policy. Use half-open intervals `[start, end)` with explicit business timezone, present human inclusive end dates only with correct conversion. Date-selection basis (service, trade, settlement, billing) is a policy choice and may be unknown.

Promotion or override operations initially mean **rate replacement**, not an implicit additive discount. Other operations require named mechanism/version, not inference. Resolution is per component: a product rate override does not erase a separate guarantee or entitlement.

### 4.3 Prepurchases and guarantees

Record payer and recipient independently (`savvy`/`advisor` are synthetic parties), currency, signed event type, coverage basis, unit, effective window, source evidence and allocation order. Broker prepays Savvy and Savvy prepays broker are distinct agreements; an advance is not automatically a loan, credit, revenue or recoverable balance. Calculate earned/billed amount independently from settled prepaid coverage, unpaid obligation, and confirmed cash. `cash_due = max(billed - eligible_prepaid_applied - other_settlement, 0)` only for a stated same-currency direction and absent other terms; handle over-credit separately, never hide it. Zero incremental cash applies only to covered liability. Support partial/exhausted coverage, transaction splitting, replay, correction and original-grant reversal. Two-way cash and balances need explicit semantics before implemented.

A guarantee specifies eligible metric and dimension filters, grouping, window/timezone, floor/ceiling, unit/currency, threshold, formula or step schedule, direction, prepaid/credit/refund treatment, stacking and true-up date. Same-unit minimum: `max(floor - eligible_measure, 0)`; unlike units require approved conversion/schedule. Period total is calculated once per guarantee group, not accumulated once per daily run. Nested guarantees require explicit hierarchy; reject ambiguous overlapping stacking. Keep expected top-up separate from earned and actual settled amounts.

### 4.4 Precision, reconciliation, time

Persist money as integer minor units when representable; unit conversion and FX are out of scope initially. Rates and quantities use fixed decimal or rational arithmetic with explicit precision; no binary floats. The agreement defines rounding mode, scale, and rounding stage; reserve fractional intermediate values; deterministic largest-remainder allocation when needed, with stable tie keys and sum invariants. Example fixtures declare their rounding, not a universal policy.

Distinguish economic event date, source observed date, system recorded timestamp, policy effective interval, service period, as-of cutoff, actual billing and settlement dates. Source and policy revisions preserve `valid` and `recorded` time. Late data creates new input/run version; posted-like demo results receive reversing adjustment lines rather than overwriting prior results. A policy's retroactive application is an explicit approved operation.

## 5. Data contracts and quality

Internal source adapter interface `load(batch_id) -> raw_manifest + records` with a deterministic synthetic adapter and a future Savvy-internal adapter, not a new broker-provided file. No implied database access, source ownership, API, CSV schema, daily availability or live connection exists. A daily cutoff or uploaded synthetic batch is a design choice. Manifest: tenant scope, source ID, batch ID, schema version, kind (snapshot/event), business date, source watermark, observed/received timestamps, record count, SHA-256 checksum, synthetic flag and completeness state. Unknown source format is preserved for review; do not parse arbitrary contracts or files automatically.

Account snapshot illustrative fields: source account key, business date, source observation timestamp, value as exact decimal, currency and freshness. Optional advisor-book attribution and dimension assignments are separate records with evidence/effective time, not assumed guaranteed on every feed. A balance difference does not prove market movement, client flow, new or lost customer. Never infer those categories without approved inputs. Source identifiers and PII stay tenant-scoped. Reject duplicates/conflicting payloads; quarantine row-level errors; partial batch is withheld unless policy explicitly permits partial publication. Empty data is not proof of no clients; stale carry-forward never becomes fresh. Prevent synthetic data entering real environments via isolated database and enforced synthetic-only flags.

## 6. Data model (PostgreSQL, proposed)

All tenant-owned tables carry `tenant_id`, UUID primary key, `created_at`, `created_by`; scoped FKs and row access control are mandatory for production; the demo has fictional tenant isolation. Datetimes are UTC plus separate policy timezone; currency is ISO code but first implementation USD only. Audit and raw evidence are append-only via application controls and restricted DB roles. Review actual production requirements before claiming immutability, security or tenancy readiness.

Evaluate query/index choices against real volume before partitioning.

## 7. APIs and error contracts

Existing `GET /api/bootstrap` and `POST /api/forecast` belong to the old demo; retain their current behavior and explicitly label its output. The rewritten application exposes a proposed GraphQL API from Rails; the following REST-like operation names are conceptual use cases and must be mapped to the GraphQL operations in the revised architecture section. No Savvy private schema has been inspected. Each request includes synthetic scope and authenticated context in any deployment beyond loopback. Never use caller-supplied `advisor_id` as authorization.

| Operation | Input and response | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/agreements` | parties, synthetic source and draft clauses → agreement ID/version | Draft only. |
| `POST /api/v1/policies/{id}/validate` | draft version → errors, conflicts, required missing source fields | Read-only validation. |
| `POST /api/v1/policies/{id}/simulate` | draft version, explicit synthetic snapshot IDs, scenarios → proposed result and diff | No activation, posting, or entitlement consumption. |
| `POST /api/v1/policies/{id}/approve` | exact agreement/policy hashes, review role, actor and decision → immutable approval | Demo only; legal approval differs from signature or execution. |
| `POST /api/v1/policies/{id}/activate` | approved version, effective interval, expected revision → version reference | Must revalidate with optimistic concurrency; user authorization required in real deployment. |
| `POST /api/v1/source-batches` | synthetic manifest and records → accepted/quarantined batch ID | Does not infer classifications. |
| `POST /api/v1/projection-runs` | snapshot, policy versions, period, as-of and scenario → run ID | Frozen trial calculation; no actual entitlement writes. |
| `GET /api/v1/runs/{id}` | run ID → state, totals, input coverage, individual line references | Read-only. |
| `GET /api/v1/runs/{id}/lines/{line_id}/trace` | line → source IDs, candidate and winning rules, arithmetic and assumptions | Read-only. |
| `POST /api/v1/actual-batches` | synthetic invoice/cash/credit/reversal evidence and revisions → batch ID | Does not post accounting or pay anyone. |
| `POST /api/v1/reconciliations` | projection run and actual evidence snapshot, matching policy → reconciliation ID | Read-only evaluation over frozen evidence. |
| `GET /api/v1/agreements/{id}/review-packet` | agreement/policy version → draft clause text, examples, unresolved terms | Export; no legal authority or signature. |

Idempotency: scoped `Idempotency-Key` plus canonical request hash. Same scope/key/hash returns original operation state/result; same scope/key with different hash returns `409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. `422 VALIDATION_ERROR` includes safe field paths and codes; `409 POLICY_CONFLICT` for concurrent modifications; `BLOCKED_MISSING_DIMENSION`, `UNSUPPORTED_MECHANISM`, `INCOMPLETE_INPUT`, `AMBIGUOUS_RULE`, `UNIT_MISMATCH` per group. A projection can expose partial groups as partial only when explicitly requested and visibly marked; a blocked calculation is never output as zero. `202` with run ID for future asynchronous execution; initial demo may execute synchronously. Authorization/error details must not reveal another tenant's data.

## 10. Test matrix and invariants

- Rule precedence: baseline 80%, promo 85%, product 90%; $1,000 eligible → $900, promo winner only absent override, outside promo fallback 80%; equally granular customer and product rules block unless priority resolves.
- Effective dates: exact half-open boundaries, timezone and recorded/valid time; re-run prior as-of versus latest corrected.
- Synthetic prepaid direction: 100 units × $2, 80 eligible prepaid units → billed $200, coverage $160, incremental cash $40; 120 covered units → $0 due and 20 remain. Repeat with payer/payee reversed; no automatic netting between directions.
- Split transaction over entitlement exhaustion, refunds, expiry and explicitly defined rollover; correct original-grant reversal; concurrent claims cannot overconsume.
- Floor: earned $70, floor $100 → $30 top-up; $120 earned → $0. $200 earned, $160 prepaid-settled, $180 floor on earnings → $0 top-up. Ceiling behavior requires explicit opposite adjustment semantics; metric/threshold mismatch blocks.
- Period grouping: A earns $50, B $150, $100 per-product floors → $50 top-up; broker-wide $200 pooled floor → $0. Nested guarantees require explicit ordering; detect cycles.
- Repeat daily projection does not accumulate full-period floors; synthetic as-of snapshots preserve past results and do not consume live entitlement.
- Corrections: $90 earnings + $10 top-up → corrected $70 earnings + $30 top-up, incremental deltas −$20 and +$20; no duplicated $30 payment. Invoice void does not imply refund, credit != cash.
- Duplicate source event same content is idempotent; same ID/revision conflicting contents quarantined; older event arriving later follows configured event-time order and may trigger revision.
- Rates and money decimal; exact sums, independent hand-worked examples, declared rounding at chosen boundary and stable penny allocation. Missing never silently becomes zero.
- Synthetic data isolation, path allowlist, no network/payment effects, access restrictions for non-demo, immutable run receipt and source trace; meaningful browser tests for the implemented controls. No production guarantee from test count.

## 12. Relationship model — customer, advisor, broker, entities

Purpose: carry customer-level economic evidence to advisor- and firm-level outputs without making names or marketing affiliations into legal entities. The public Savvy disclosures distinguish Savvy Wealth Inc. from registered investment adviser Savvy Advisors, Inc.; a separate broker-dealer's April 2026 disclosure describes common control. These are public entity descriptions, not permission to assume accounting books, relationships, data availability or actual integration. Savvy's website describes some DBAs as marketing identities rather than separate registered entities. Sources: https://www.savvywealth.com/disclosure and https://files.brokercheck.finra.org/crs_338136.pdf . Use fictional legal entities and roles in the prototype.

Tenant/workspace is an authorization scope; legal entity is an accounting/reporting boundary. They are not the same. A party may assume multiple roles with explicit effective dates; customer, advisor, broker-dealer, custodian, payee and legal entity are not synonyms. Account holders may include trusts, joint owners or other organizations. Household is a grouping, not automatically a customer, contract obligor or account owner. A practice/DBA is a brand/business grouping, not automatically a legal entity. External custodian identifiers are provenance/crosswalks, never a broker-provided mandatory downstream file.

## 13. Economic facts and accounting granularity

Retain it as a firm-level fact; if Finance approves a supported allocation, store algorithm, evidence, weights, rounding and allocated/estimated flag, and do not label as source-observed customer revenue. No percentages may be inferred from balances without policy.

Advisor split expresses economics; it does not prove the company is principal or agent or determine gross vs net reporting. Customer asset balances/AUM are client property/operational inputs, not automatically Savvy assets or revenue.

## 18. Broker-led application with customer-level cash collection

**Relationship and contract design:** broker `B` has dated association records to customers `C1...Cn`. Customer, account, legal entity and service/contract are distinct; a broker commercial agreement and customer service agreement remain separate. A relationship records role (servicing vs compensation beneficiary), `valid_from`, exclusive `valid_to`, `recorded_at`, evidence, owner and, where required, approved allocation. For multiple brokers or mid-period transfers, never infer a single owner or allocation; block affected rollup until approved. A service engagement identifies the legal contracting customer/payer, provider entity, period, currency and customer contract version. Commercial ownership/payment attribution dates and basis are explicit policies, not necessarily payment date or current servicing advisor. Contract-specific terms override only their defined scope.

**Collections model:** customer invoices/charges live at customer × contract × service period × entity × currency × billing line grain when available. Record payment receipts by stable source/processor ID and actual payer, currency, effective/observed timestamps, type and state; customer obligations get explicit payment applications with line amount and source evidence. A customer may have several invoices; a receipt can be split over obligations under documented policy. Store unapplied cash as unapplied to a customer if identity is supported; unknown payer remains unallocated at entity/clearing scope. A single invoice may have multiple partial applications. Credits, write-offs, voids and refunds are distinct from cash receipts. Customer-level cash is not created by prorating a broker aggregate or treating a credit as cash. Preserve original payment applications and reversal links; corrections create revised evidence, never rewrite a finalized snapshot.

**Three separate matches:** (1) customer obligation → customer-level payment application; (2) payment/processor batch → bank deposit, including explicit fees/refunds/timing/clearing; (3) customer activity/receipt → effective-dated broker allocation under an approved rule. A bank settlement may aggregate multiple customers; by itself it is not customer-level proof. No application or broker rollup is considered complete without sufficient source and mapping evidence. Recognition and advisor payment statuses remain independent from collection. Customer cash rollups use an approved declared basis (e.g., attributed by underlying service date versus collection date); never imply collection-triggered compensation is an established policy. Explain why a specific broker received a share when relationships change.

Customer drilldown shows contract, line-level bill, cash applications, payment/bank-match status and Campfire objects with separate accounting states. Brokers who are themselves a contractual customer may have direct customer obligations as a separate explicit party role; broker-to-Savvy prepayment and Savvy-to-broker advance or payout remain separately directional and must not be netted into customer collections by default. Bank reconciliation and revenue recognition remain intended accountant workflows in Campfire where supported; our app preserves operational attribution, review links and the evidence trail. Aggregate monetary totals only within the same entity/currency/period; unknown/stale records remain visible. UI role boundaries require approval; no assumption every broker may view every customer detail.
